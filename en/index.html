The studio

Loro's Games is two friends who love games. "Loro" means parrot in Spanish: hence our mascot, a chatty macaw who never lets go of his controller.

We love games you understand in three seconds and keep playing for hours. Each game has its own style: pixel art, gothic claymation, 19th-century engravings…
